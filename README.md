## 📌 About ReclaimR

Losing personal belongings on a college campus is a common problem. Finding them again can be difficult because there is often no centralized platform where students and staff can report lost or found items.

**ReclaimR** is a campus-focused item recovery system designed to make the process of reporting, discovering, and recovering lost belongings simpler and more organized.

The platform provides a centralized place where users can browse lost and found items and eventually connect owners with finders.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional campus Lost & Found systems often depend on:

- WhatsApp groups
- Notice boards
- Informal communication
- Asking friends or classmates

These methods make it difficult to:

- Track lost and found items
- Search for specific belongings
- Maintain organized records
- Connect owners with finders
- Know whether a lost item has been recovered

ReclaimR aims to provide a **centralized digital solution for campus item recovery**.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ReclaimR provides a single platform where users can:

- 📝 Report lost items
- 📦 Report found items
- 🔎 Browse lost and found listings
- 🏫 View items associated with their campus
- 📍 Provide item and location details
- 🤝 Connect owners and finders
- ⚡ Quickly identify potentially matching items
